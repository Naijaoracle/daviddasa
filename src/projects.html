Artificial Intelligence in Medicine · Sep 2025 · DOI: 10.1016/j.artmed.2025.103270

DASEX Framework & XR-NPC Clinical Simulator

The DASEX framework is the first structured methodology for evaluating AI-driven non-player characters in XR healthcare simulations, published in Artificial Intelligence in Medicine. The accompanying XR-NPC simulator embeds multi-agent AI clinical colleagues into virtual ward environments for team-based training, enabling safe and repeatable high-stakes scenario practice.

  • XR Healthcare Simulation
  • AI-Driven NPCs

medRxiv · Oct 2025 · 306 participants · Nigeria

rPPG Equity Study

A field study investigating remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) for contactless blood pressure screening, with 306 participants recruited in Nigeria. The study exposes skin tone bias in existing models and provides evidence for equity-focused design of remote monitoring tools for underserved populations.

  • Remote Photoplethysmography
  • Hypertension Screening
  • Health Equity
