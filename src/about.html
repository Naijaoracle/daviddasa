David Dasa

Medical Doctor | PhD Researcher | Digital Health & AI

I'm a Medical Doctor with seven years of clinical experience across Nigeria and the UK, currently pursuing a PhD at Bournemouth University on Advanced NPC Systems for XR Healthcare Simulation. My work produces peer-reviewed research at the intersection of AI, Extended Reality, and clinical equity — including a published evaluation framework and a field study on remote blood pressure screening bias conducted with 306 participants in Nigeria.
